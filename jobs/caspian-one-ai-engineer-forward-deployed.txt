About the role

Caspian One has partnered with a forward thinking AI Start Up looking for an experienced AI Engineer to join their delivery ecosystem, with a strong preference for engineers based in Toronto or the surrounding region.

This role sits at the intersection of engineering, client delivery, and real world problem solving. You will be responsible for taking AI solutions from concept through to production, working closely with customers on-site to integrate systems, navigate complexity, and ensure successful adoption.

This is not a research role. It is a hands on delivery position focused on building, deploying, and scaling AI solutions that solve tangible business problems.

What You Will Be Doing

Designing and deploying AI driven workflows using large language models (LLMs) and agent based architectures
Working directly with client systems to connect, map, and contextualise distributed data sources without heavy data ingestion
Building context layers that reflect business rules, proprietary data, and operational processes
Developing production ready solutions that integrate with existing enterprise environments
Collaborating with clients through the full lifecycle, from initial use case definition through to deployment and adoption
Handling complex delivery scenarios including change management, stakeholder alignment, and technical challenges
Supporting rapid delivery cycles, typically delivering working solutions within 6 to 12 weeks
Contributing to ongoing expansion by identifying additional use cases and opportunities within existing clients
Ensuring solutions meet defined service levels where managed applications are required
